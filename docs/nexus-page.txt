=== NAME ===
DAGGER - Dark's Adaptive Goam & GoamEER Ear Retractor

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (tagline) ===
Hides Goam's Elven Ears and Goam's Elven Ears Redone under hoods, cowls and helmets, and shows them again when you take the headwear off. SKSE plugin, no ESP, no scripts.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
[size=5][b]DAGGER[/b][/size]
[i]Dark's Adaptive Goam & GoamEER Ear Retractor[/i]

Long elven ears poking through a hood are a classic clipping problem. DAGGER fixes it for [b]Goam's Elven Ears[/b] and [b]Goam's Elven Ears Redone[/b]: put on a hood, cowl or helmet and the ears disappear; take it off and they are back.

[size=4][b]How it works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]It hides the ear meshes on your character's loaded model. It does not swap head parts and does not rebuild the head, so the change is immediate.
[*]No ESP and no Papyrus scripts. Nothing is written to your save game, and load order does not matter.
[*]Works with Goam's Elven Ears, Goam's Elven Ears Redone, or both installed together.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What counts as covered[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Anything in the Head (slot 30) or Hair (slot 31) armor slot: vanilla hoods, cowls, hats and helmets.
[*]Circlets do [b]not[/b] hide the ears.
[*]Player character only.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Skyrim SE / AE
[*][url=https://skse.silverlock.org/]SKSE64[/url]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444]Address Library for SKSE Plugins[/url]
[*]Goam's Elven Ears and/or Goam's Elven Ears Redone (not included)
[/list]
Tested on game version 1.7.104. Other SE/AE versions should work through Address Library but are untested.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Install with Vortex or Mod Organizer 2. The FOMOD asks which ears mod you use (Redone, Original or Both) and pre-selects the one that matches your active plugins.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
Optional: [font=Courier New]SKSE/Plugins/DAGGER.ini[/font]
[list]
[*][b]prefixes[/b] - ear geometry name prefixes to hide.
[*][b]slotMask[/b] - 3 = Head and Hair (default), 1 = Head only.
[*][b]ignoreCirclets[/b] - skip armor with the ClothingCirclet keyword (default on).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/size]
The log is [font=Courier New]Documents\My Games\Skyrim Special Edition\SKSE\DAGGER.log[/font]. It lists every item you equip with its slot mask and which ear meshes were found. If a hood from another mod does not hide the ears, check its slot in the log.

[size=4][b]Known limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Player only (no followers or NPCs).
[*]A mod that rebuilds your head model while covered can briefly show the ears; DAGGER re-applies the hide within a quarter of a second.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Uninstalling[/b][/size]
Remove the mod. Nothing is stored in your save.
